Food safety comes first

Last Tray coordinates handoffs; it does not inspect food or guarantee it is safe to eat.

Donors are responsible for describing food accurately, including allergens, storage conditions, preparation time, and use-by guidance. Recipients should only accept food they can handle safely under local rules. When in doubt, don’t redistribute it.

This demo uses sample listings. For real-world use, food safety rules and donation protections vary by location; confirm the local requirements before operating.
